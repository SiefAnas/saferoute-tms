# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **SafeTurns LLC**, a Massachusetts limited liability company ("SafeTurns", "we", "us"), handles information in the SafeTurns website, web application and mobile applications (the "Service"). It should be read together with our [Terms of Use](/terms).

## 1. Our role

SafeTurns provides school transportation software to transport companies (each a "Customer"). The Customer, and the schools it serves, decide whose information goes into the Service and why. The information they and their users enter ("Customer Data") belongs to the Customer.

For Customer Data, SafeTurns acts as a service provider on the Customer's behalf: we process it to run the Service for that Customer and according to its instructions and any written agreement with it. If you have a question about why your information, or your child's information, is in the Service, please contact the transport company or school that added it. You can also contact us and we will pass your request on.

## 2. Our promises

- **We do not sell personal information**, and we do not share it for advertising.
- **We do not use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking tools** in the Service.
- **We do not use student information to build profiles** or for any purpose other than providing the Service.
- **Students do not have accounts.** Students do not use the Service. Their information is entered by the transport company, the school, or a parent or guardian.

## 3. Information in the Service

Depending on how the Customer uses the Service, it may contain:

- **Students:** first and last name, school, grade, student ID number assigned by the school or company, home address and other pickup or drop-off addresses, pickup and drop-off times and days, parent or guardian contacts, short operational care notes (for example that a student needs a wheelchair, a car seat, or a monitor), assignment to a driver and van, and a record of trips, attendance, skipped pickups and no-shows.
- **Parents and guardians:** name, email address, phone number, address, the students they are linked to, and requests they send (for example skipping a pickup).
- **Drivers and monitors:** name, email address, phone number, home address, driver license number, van and route assignments, shift check-in and check-out times, and, where the driver or monitor allows it, the device location at the moment of check-in or check-out. Pay rates, adjustments, and pay periods that the Customer enters for its own payroll purposes.
- **School administrators and staff:** name, email address, phone number, school, and which students they have been given access to.
- **Company administrators:** name, email address, phone number, and company details.
- **Vehicles:** make, model, color, year, license plate, maintenance, mileage and fuel records.
- **Account and technical information:** sign-in email, a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form), when you accepted these documents, sign-in times, device push-notification tokens, and server logs that include IP addresses and request details used to keep the Service secure and working.

**What should not be entered.** The Service is not designed for medical or clinical records. Customers agree not to enter medical diagnoses, IEP documents, medication names or dosages, Social Security numbers or similar highly sensitive information. Where a student has a medical consideration, only a short operational flag should be recorded. We may remove information that goes beyond what is needed to run the route.

## 4. How we use information

We use information only to:

- provide the Service: show routes, assignments, schedules and trips to the people who need them, send notifications, and support check-in, check-out and attendance;
- create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, and reset passwords;
- provide support and respond to requests;
- keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, and investigate problems;
- maintain and improve the Service; and
- comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We may create aggregated or anonymized statistics about how the Service is used (for example, the average number of route changes per week). These statistics do not identify any Customer, student, parent, driver, monitor or other person.

## 5. Who can see information

Inside the Service, access depends on role. A transport company sees its own data. A school sees only the students it has been linked to. A driver or monitor sees the students on their own assignments. A parent sees only their own children. One company or school cannot see another's data.

We share information outside SafeTurns only:

- **With service providers that run the Service for us**, under obligations to protect it and use it only for that purpose. Currently these are: Neon (database hosting, United States), Render (application hosting, United States), Resend (email delivery), Expo (delivery of push notifications to the mobile app), and the push-notification services built into web browsers and mobile operating systems (for example those operated by Apple, Google and Mozilla), which carry the notification text to your device.
- **When required by law**, for example to respond to a valid subpoena or court order, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to protect someone's safety.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy continues to apply to the information transferred.
- **With the Customer**, because the Customer Data is the Customer's.

## 6. Where information is stored

Information is stored and processed in the United States.

## 7. How we protect information

We maintain reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a company of our size, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between your device, our servers and our database, role-based access controls that limit each user to their own organization's data, hashed passwords, forced password changes for temporary passwords, and limits on repeated sign-in attempts. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information.

**If something goes wrong.** If we become aware of unauthorized access to or disclosure of Customer Data, we will notify the affected Customer without unreasonable delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, share what we know, and cooperate with the Customer's response. We will also meet our own obligations under applicable law, including Massachusetts General Laws chapter 93H.

## 8. How long we keep information

- We keep Customer Data for as long as the Customer uses the Service.
- When a Customer closes its account, all of its users are signed out right away. The account can be restored for thirty (30) days, and after that the Customer Data is deleted from our live systems.
- When a Customer's written agreement with us ends, or when a Customer asks us in writing, we delete its Customer Data from our live systems within thirty (30) days and, if asked, first give the Customer an export in a common format.
- Copies in routine backups are deleted as those backups expire on their normal cycle.
- We may keep information longer where the law requires it, or where it is needed to resolve a dispute or enforce our agreements, and only for as long as needed.

The Customer, as the employer of its drivers and monitors and as the transportation operator, is responsible for keeping any records the law requires it to keep. It should export them before closing its account.

## 9. Your choices and rights

- **See or correct your information.** You can see and update much of your own information on your Account page. For other changes, contact your organization's administrator.
- **Delete your information.** You can ask for your account to be deleted from your Account page, or by writing to us. Because Customer Data belongs to the Customer, we will usually work with your organization to handle the request, and some records may need to be kept as described in Section 8.
- **Parents and guardians** who want to review, correct or delete their child's information should contact the transport company or school, or write to us and we will pass the request on and help.
- **Notifications and location.** You can turn off push notifications and location access in your device settings at any time.

We will respond to requests within forty-five (45) days. We may need to verify your identity first. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's law; contact us to exercise them. We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

## 10. Children's privacy

The Service is meant for adults acting for a transport company or a school, and for parents and guardians. Children do not create accounts or use the Service, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Information about students is entered by the transport company, the school, or a parent or guardian, and the Customer is responsible for obtaining any consent required. Where a school or district is subject to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), we handle student information only under the direction of the Customer and its schools, and only to provide the Service. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Cookies and browser storage

The web application stores your sign-in token and a few display preferences in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you clear your browser storage, you will be signed out.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. When we make a material change, we will post the new version here with a new version number and effective date, and where appropriate notify you in the Service or by email.
